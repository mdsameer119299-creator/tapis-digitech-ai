What It Does

A Smarter First Line for Your Business

Your receptionist should help customers move forward, not simply send every call to voicemail. We design voice workflows around your business, team and customer journey.

Answer Incoming Calls

Provide a consistent first response when your team is busy, unavailable or outside normal working hours.

Qualify Enquiries

Ask the questions that matter to your business and capture useful information before a human follow-up.

Route the Conversation

FAQ

AI Voice Receptionist Questions

Can an AI receptionist transfer calls to people?

Yes. Call routing and human handoff can be designed as part of the workflow, depending on the phone and integration setup.

Can it qualify leads?

Yes. The workflow can ask predefined questions and capture the information your team needs for follow-up.

Can it work after business hours?

Yes. After-hours handling can be included when the underlying telephony setup supports it.

Will it replace my staff?

The goal is to automate suitable repetitive interactions while giving your team clear escalation and handoff paths for conversations that need people.
