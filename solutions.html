FAQ

Solutions, Answered

How is a ‘solution’ different from a service?

Services are the disciplines we practise — AI, software, cloud, marketing. A solution combines the right services around one business outcome you care about, like cutting costs or increasing revenue. You buy the result, not the parts.

Do you build custom, or configure off-the-shelf?

Both, pragmatically. Where a proven platform fits, we configure and integrate it to save you time and money. Where you need an edge, we build custom. We recommend whichever delivers the outcome fastest and cheapest to own.

How do you measure success?

We agree the metric up front — hours saved, cost reduced, conversion lifted, tickets deflected — instrument it, and report against it. If it doesn’t move the number, it isn’t done.

How quickly can we see results?

Most outcome-focused engagements start with a short discovery sprint, then ship a first working slice within weeks so you see value early and we de-risk the rest.
