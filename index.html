Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about working with TAPIS DIGITECH.

What does TAPIS DIGITECH do?
TAPIS DIGITECH is an enterprise AI & digital transformation company. We design, build and scale production-grade AI agents, custom software, websites, mobile apps, cloud infrastructure, automation and digital marketing — all around measurable business outcomes.
Do you build custom AI agents and automation?
Yes. We build custom AI agents, chatbots, voice AI and workflow automation that reason over your data, call your tools and complete real tasks — with human-in-the-loop controls on anything sensitive.
Which industries do you work with?
We work across finance, healthcare, retail & e-commerce, manufacturing and more — tailoring AI, software and automation to each sector's data, compliance and growth needs.
Do you work with clients globally?
Yes. We're based in India and deliver for clients worldwide, including the US, UK, UAE, Singapore and Australia, with transparent, sprint-based delivery.
How quickly can we get started?
Discovery can begin within days. Our sprint-based process is designed to deliver first value in weeks, with measurable checkpoints at every stage.
Is your AI secure and responsible?
Security and responsible AI are built in from day one: encryption, least-privilege access, evaluation harnesses and human oversight — so you can scale with confidence and a full audit trail.
