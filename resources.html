Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What services does TAPIS DIGITECH offer?

We cover AI solutions, software and app development, digital marketing, branding & design, and cloud & IT services — end to end, under one roof.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A focused landing page or chatbot can launch in 2–3 weeks, while a full platform or app usually runs 8–16 weeks. We give a clear timeline after discovery.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with clients across India and internationally, and adapt our process to your time zone and industry.

How much does a project cost?

Every engagement is scoped to your needs, so pricing varies. Book a free consultation and we will give you a transparent, itemised estimate.

Do you provide support after launch?

Absolutely. We offer ongoing support, maintenance, training and optimisation plans so your solution keeps performing and improving.

Can you work with our existing team and tools?

Yes — we integrate with the platforms you already use and can collaborate directly with your in-house team.
